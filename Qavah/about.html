Our Story

No Template.
Just Tides.

Qavah is a Hebrew meaning "to wait, to hope, to be bound together in expectation." We are a ministry raising a generation that has stopped rushing God and started waiting on Him on purpose.

Where We Started

Born In A
Waiting Room.

Qavah began as a small gathering of believers who were tired of formulas and tired of altars that rushed to a result before the presence of God had even shown up. What started as a handful of people choosing to wait together has grown into gatherings, conferences and a worship sound that Lagos now recognises on sight.

We are not a denomination. We are not a five-step program to your breakthrough. We are a people who believe the wait itself is where strength is forged and we build every service, every conference, every note sung around that one conviction.

Prayer That Waits

Unhurried altars where presence is pursued before requests are made.

Worship That Waits

Sound as sacrifice with voices raised until heaven visibly answers.

A People Made Ready

Discipleship for a generation strengthened, not shortcut, by the process.

There is no template in Qavah. We go by the tides, by the will of the wind.

Founder & Visionary

Abbey
Ojomu

Abbey Ojomu is the visionary behind Qavah Evangelical Ministry. She is a voice raised in worship and a life given to the discipline of waiting on God. Her conviction is simple and unshifting: prayer doesn't kill, and the longer the wait, the more strengthened you become.

What began as a personal pursuit of God's presence has grown, under her leadership, into a movement with gatherings like qavah and conferences like The Glory of God, each one built not on formula but on following the tides, by the will of the wind.

Abbey leads Qavah as both minister and worshipper, believing every altar should leave people not just prayed for, but strengthened.

"Prayer doesn't kill. When you wait, there is a result. That result is called strength."
Abbey Ojomu
