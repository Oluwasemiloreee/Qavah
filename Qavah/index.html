Welcome To Qavah Evangelical Ministry

Those who
Wait shall
renew.

Qavah is a Hebrew word meaning "to wait, to hope, to be bound together in expectation." We are a ministry raising a generation that waits on God on purpose: in prayer, in worship, and in the fire that refuses to go out.

"They that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles." — Isaiah 40:31

Who We Are

No Template.
Just Tides.

Qavah is not built to a formula. We move by the tides, by the will of the wind gathering believers who are done rushing God and ready to be strengthened in the wait. Every service, every conference, every note sung is an act of patient, expectant faith.

Prayer That Waits

Unhurried altars where presence is pursued before requests are made.

Worship That Waits

Sound as sacrifice with voices raised until heaven visibly answers.

A People Made Ready

Discipleship for a generation strengthened, not shortcut, by the process.
